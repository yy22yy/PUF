During our experiments, we found the attack to work in

surprising, as the MLP attack, in contrast to the LR attack,
is not restricted to functions of a certain class. Nevertheless,

Experiments using our MLP-based implementation of the
splitting attack showed that a 64-bit (1,7)-Interpose PUF

CRPs (i.e., with data complexity less than previously reported
for the (1,7) case).
